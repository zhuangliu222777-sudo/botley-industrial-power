Customization scope

Built around the requirement that makes your product different.

OEM projects are reviewed against electrical feasibility, production volume, destination-market requirements and the level of engineering change involved.

01

Electrical configuration

Input and output voltage, phase arrangement, frequency, capacity, regulation accuracy, overload profile and redundancy strategy.

02

Protection & control

Protection thresholds, bypass logic, alarms, metering, communications, remote monitoring interfaces and control sequences.

03

Mechanical design

Cabinet dimensions, cable entry, ventilation, enclosure protection, internal layout, color and installation constraints.

04

Brand presentation

Nameplate, product label, cabinet graphics, model coding, user interface language and customer-supplied brand assets.

05

Documentation

Datasheets, operation manuals, wiring diagrams, test records, packing information and market-specific language requirements.

06

Testing & delivery

Agreed inspection points, functional testing, output verification, acceptance records and packaging for the destination.

OEM workflow

A controlled path from specification to repeat production.

Requirement review

Confirm the target product, market, electrical data, interfaces, branding and expected quantity.

Technical proposal

Define the configuration, deviations from the standard platform and the information still required.

Sample approval

Agree drawings, labels and documentation, then verify the initial unit against the approved specification.

Production release

Freeze the approved configuration and inspection requirements before repeat manufacturing.
