Start with your load and grid data.

Capacity alone is not enough. Starting current, load type, voltage fluctuation, phase arrangement, environment and redundancy targets all influence a reliable selection.

Useful information to share

  • Rated and peak load in kW or kVA
  • Input voltage range, phase and frequency
  • Required output voltage and tolerance
  • Load type and startup characteristics
  • Installation environment and duty cycle
